About

I work on questions at the intersection of international trade, firm behaviour, and technological change. The common theme of my research is the question how firms adapt when their economic environment changes. I study what they choose to produce, how their organisation changes, and what technologies they adopt. I approach these questions empirically and theoretically.

Current Interests

My current research studies different forms of firm adjustment to changes in their economic environment. In my Job Market Paper, I study how offshoring and access to lower-cost inputs affect firms' technology choices, and particularly if they operate across a wide range of output technologies, or focus on a narrow niche. A second project analyses the exports of large multiproduct firms and how these firms organise products into systems, centred around a focal main product. In the third chapter of my dissertation, we study how corporate taxation interacts with firm ownership, examining whether firms with different types of owners respond differently to tax policy and whether tax changes affect ownership structures themselves.
